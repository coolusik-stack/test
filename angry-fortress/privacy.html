Dotori Fortress Privacy Policy

Effective: September 27, 2026

Dotori Fortress does not collect or store personal information such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, location, contacts or device identifiers. There are no accounts, ads or analytics.

Settings, win/loss records and tutorial progress are stored only on your device and are deleted with the app.

When you play a friend match, a 4-letter room code and temporary connection information (such as your IP address) are sent to a connection broker (the public PeerJS server) and a connectivity helper (Google STUN) solely to connect the two devices. Game data during the match travels directly between the two devices; the developer does not receive or store it.
